Will these two audiences actually click?

Taste-Fit takes two brands (or a brand and an artist, podcast, venue…) and asks Qloo Taste AI what their fans share, where they differ, and what fans of both over-index on. It scores the fit, maps the joint audience in a city, and writes a brief where every claim cites a Qloo call.

        How it works

        1 · Resolve

        /search turns free text into Qloo entities (brands, artists, places, podcasts…).

        2 · Compare

        filter.results.entities asks Qloo how much each audience likes the other side (both directions); urn:tag insights say what they share; urn:demographics gives age and gender indices; /v2/analysis/compare shows which catalog tags the two entities share. The score is a printed formula, not a vibe.

        3 · Activate

        Joint-signal /v2/insights with explainability finds artists, podcasts and places that fans of both over-index on; urn:heatmap shows where in the city.

        4 · Write

        An LLM agent (Claude, with a scripted fallback) can pull more evidence through the same tools, then must submit the brief through a strict schema.

        5 · Verify

        Every citation and named entity is checked against the ledger. Anything Qloo did not return is removed and flagged.

        Limits

        Affinities are aggregate audience signals, not facts about people. Demo data is clearly badged. The hosted demo is rate-limited to respect the event quota.
